OneContext: Brand Tone & Copy Guide
Brand Positioning
OneContext is the Stripe for AI Identity—developer-first, open source, and essential infrastructure.
We solve the real, tedious pain of setting up your personal context in every AI tool. Our users are power users: developers, founders, and creators running multiple AI interfaces daily—ChatGPT, Claude, OpenClaw, Cursor, and others. We're leveraging OpenClaw's breakout momentum but building for long-haul infrastructure. OneContext is not another AI chatbot; we are the identity layer that all AI tools can plug into.

Brand Voice & Personality
Voice: Direct, technical (but never obscure), confident but humble, solution-obsessed, and absolutely anti-BS.
We never oversell or hype. We identify problems, deliver solutions, and move on.
Personality: That sharp, pragmatic developer friend who built what they needed and open sourced it because it should exist. Slightly opinionated, fundamentally helpful, always respects user intelligence.
Tone shifts:
Technical docs: Precise, complete, matter-of-fact.
Landing page: Punchy and sharply benefit-driven.
Social media: Conversational, meme-aware, always genuine.
Always: Clear over clever. Specific over fluffy.
Messaging Framework

Set up your AI identity once. Use it everywhere.

You're burning time repeating your bio and context in every new AI tool. You forget to update them and the context drifts out of sync.
Solution:
OneContext auto-syncs your digital footprint (X/Twitter, GitHub, Notion, and more) into a single profile you can use with any AI tool—via MCP or API.
Key Benefits:
Stop repeating yourself.
Always current (auto daily updates).
Works everywhere (MCP + API).

Copy Principles
Lead with the pain: Name the real frustration up front.
Be explicit: “Stop copy-pasting your bio into ChatGPT, Claude, and OpenClaw”—never “Improve your AI workflow.”
Show, don’t tell: Use code snippets, specific screenshots, and actual integration names.
Respect technical audience: Never dumb it down. Complexity is okay—just don’t hide it.
Short, scannable sentences:
No fluff. Every word earns its place.
Avoid ‘AI slop language’:
Never use: unlock, supercharge, revolutionize, seamless, game-changer, next-generation, empower, leverage, synergy.

Words We Use / Words We Avoid


Use	Avoid	Reason
Identity, context	Unlock, supercharge, revolutionize	Direct over hype
Profile, sync	Transform, seamless*, game-changer	No AI slop
Auto-update, connect	Next-generation, empower, leverage(v)	Specific verbs, not vague
Integrate, MCP, API	Synergy, ecosystem*	*Only if used literally (AI tool ecosystem)
Open source, control	Robust, scalable*, innovative*	Only for technical docs if necessary
Developer-first	Surprise & delight	Be technical, not fluffy
Privacy-first, self-host	Disruptive, magical	Concrete, neutral language
Social Media Voice
Twitter/X:
Conversational, developer-savvy, occasionally meme-driven. Use storytelling threads: “I got tired of setting up my context in every AI tool…” Share code snippets, integration guides, and user wins. Engage the OpenClaw, Cursor, and ChatGPT communities. Retweet users and contributors.
Tone: Helpful, not salesy. ‘Here’s what we built,’ not ‘Check out our amazing product.’
GitHub:
Professional and thorough. Welcome new contributors. Answer issues and PRs with technical depth and respect. README is the chief marketing asset—make it visual, concrete, and approachable.
Discord/Community:
Supportive and patient. Prioritize technical accuracy, but meet users where they are. Share roadmaps and changelogs openly.
Reddit:
Authentic, technical, and highly context-sensitive. Only post where OneContext genuinely adds value to the conversation (e.g., r/ClaudeAI, r/LocalLLaMA, r/opensource). Lead with code and specificity, not promotion.
